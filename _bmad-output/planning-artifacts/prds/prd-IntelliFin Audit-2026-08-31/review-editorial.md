# Editorial Review — IntelliFin Audit PRD (rev 2) and Addendum

**Purpose/audience read:** this document exists to help product, engineering (architect), UX, and audit stakeholders turn the IntelliFin Audit PoC thesis into testable requirements — specifically to let an architect re-derive the spine and a UX designer re-derive the screen set from revision 2. Reader type: humans. Style guide: none stated; the document's own conventions (defined capitalized nouns, `[ASSUMPTION]` / `[NOTE FOR PM]` / `[NON-GOAL for PoC]` tags, "Consequences (testable)" schema) are treated as the baseline and preserved.

**Structure model applied:** Strategic/Context (Pyramid) for the PRD — thesis first, supporting groups below, MECE groups, evidence supports. The addendum is judged as Reference/Database — random access, consistent schema per item.

**Scope of this review:** editorial only. Content, scope, and product decisions are not challenged. Where two passages contradict each other, the finding is tagged QUESTION and asks the author to pick one; no resolution is proposed.

## Closing

Total: 41 findings (structure 16 incl. 1 preserve; prose 25). Nothing is critical: the document's shape, schema, and defined vocabulary are sound, and a downstream architect or UX designer can work from it today. The eight high findings are the ones that would send them in the wrong direction — two state-model consistency questions (Completed→Inconclusive; the Rejected review state), the §0 parenthetical that reads as "revision 2 has no Builder", the *Auditor Review* naming trap, the Non-Goals/Deferred overlap, FR-31 pointing at the glossary for a test rule, the FR-9 derivation sentence, and the FR-20 opening sentence. All eight are fixable in under an hour without touching scope.
