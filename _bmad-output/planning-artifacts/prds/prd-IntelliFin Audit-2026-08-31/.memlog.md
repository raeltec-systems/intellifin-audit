- (decision) CORRECTION to the previous entry (owner, 2026-09-11): there is NO new entity and no PRD revision needed. Every institution tracks its risks and the controls that mitigate them in some governing document — a risk register, a risk assessment register, a risk matrix; the name and format differ by institution — and that document is what this product already calls a TEMPLATE. In production a Template would be shaped around whatever the institution uses. The owner asked that no institution-specific acronym for it appear anywhere in code, copy or documents; the product word is Template. The work is therefore to make the existing Templates more structured, framed around risk, control, objective and criterion reference: ProcedureTemplate already carries objective for all four and controlStatement for P-1 only, so risk and criterion reference are the gap. Mechanically, a new Template field means editing addendum SectionC first, because the Template constants are pinned to it verbatim by procedure-templates.test.ts. Freezing is already handled: initialDraftSections copies Template text into the version's sections at creation and sections is part of FrozenPlanInputs, so a later Template edit cannot retroactively change an approved version. Story 2.15 is ordinary work with no blocker.
