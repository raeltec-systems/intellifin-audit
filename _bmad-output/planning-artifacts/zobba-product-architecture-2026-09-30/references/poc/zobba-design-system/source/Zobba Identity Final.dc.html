What Pair means

Zobba's work is always a relationship: a claim and its evidence, the conversation and the artifact, the auditor and Zobba, what was expected and what actually happened, a source and the conclusion drawn from it. The symbol is two forms held against the same line, offset so that neither completes the other. Zobba's job is to bring them level, and to show you where they don't agree.

The same pair runs through the wordmark (the two b's, the second one stepped down), the working state (the forms move toward each other while Zobba compares) and the layout (conversation beside the work). The accent is Iris, chosen because it is the one hue family audit semantics don't use.

Logo

Symbol geometry · 32-unit grid
Forms. Two 11 × 18 units. Outer corners radius 5.5 (fully round), inner corners radius 1.2, so each form has one flat face. Channel. The flat faces meet a shared centre line, 2 units apart. It widens to 2.8 at 16px and 3.6 at 12px and below, so the forms never merge. Offset. 6 units. The tinted band is the 12 units where the forms overlap: where the two things are compared. Not. Two separate pills (a pause button), links, quotation marks or two figures. The flat inner faces and the step prevent all four readings.
Sizes · 96, 48, 32, 16, 10 (provenance)
Full colour · one colour · light · dark
One colour is the default for print, provenance footers, OS menu-bar and notification templates. Full colour is for app icon, sidebar and marketing.
Wordmark · title case, stepped bb
Outlined from Hanken Grotesk 600, tracked −2.8%, the bb set tighter than the rest. The second b keeps half of its ascender above x-height: the same step as the symbol, still read as bb. Supplied as SVG; a type designer should redraw the final letterforms.

Decision: keep Hanken Grotesk
Tested against the Big Four and bank concern, it holds up once three things change. Title case replaces lowercase. UI tops out at weight 600, never 700. Tracking is only tightened at 20px and above. The start-up feel in the exploration came from the lowercase wordmark and heavy weights, not from the typeface. It stays warm: open apertures, a friendly a and g, and good figures for tables. It is open source (OFL), with no licensing cost across desktop, web and PDF. No serif. Documentary authority comes from the firm's own template, not from Zobba. Rules. One family in chrome. IBM Plex Mono only for identifiers and references, never for labels. Reading measure 60–75 characters. Tabular figures in tables. The artifact uses the methodology's fonts.

Motion · the pair as working state

IdleOffset, still. The two things are apart: nothing has been compared yet.Sidebar, Home, provenance.
WorkingThe forms slide toward level, hold, and return: a 3.6s cycle, 3 units of travel, eased. It's the motion of checking one thing against another.Always beside the activity text: "Comparing 23 leavers against 1,516 accounts". Only the current step animates.
Waiting for youLevel and still, right form open. Zobba has done its half; the other half is your decision. No pulse, no colour change."Zobba needs your permission", "Zobba needs your input".
CompleteSettles once from level back to the offset rest, then stops. Complete means the work finished, not that the control passed.Plays once per task. The result chip carries the outcome.
Quiet enough for four hours. One animated mark per view, 14px in work screens. Respects reduced-motion: working becomes level and still, with the activity text unchanged. Never encoded: pass, exception, inconclusive. The mark has no red, green or amber state, and no ✓ appears inside or beside it. Activity text does the work. Present tense with a real object: Reading control documentation · Checking population completeness · Reviewing sign-in activity · Preparing working paper · Validating conclusions. No percentages.

Desktop screens

Home. Symbol, greeting, composer shown focused (iris border and wash ring). Engagement and permissions as plain chips. Continue list with state glyphs. No tiles, no in-app marketing.
Active work. The brand is three 14px working marks and one iris chip. Guide through the composer, Stop beside it, Inspect on the current step. The limitation uses the coverage glyph, not amber.
Working paper and Changes. The page is the firm's template (Arial, firm reference scheme). Zobba contributes only the iris selection, the typographic diff, citations and the evidence list. Prepared by names Daniel.
Waiting for permission. One decision surface with sender, recipient, email, meeting time, calendar and invitees. The waiting mark is still and neutral. Graphite "Allow and send"; the drafts are open beside it for checking.
Scheduled check. A one-colour mark identifies the actor ("Zobba ran this unattended"). The result chip is visually independent: dashed, neutral, half-filled. Readable conclusion first, details folded.
Voice in these screens Chrome: Zobba is analysing · Zobba needs your permission · Zobba ran this unattended · Zobba completed the weekly check. Conversation: "I found the control definition…" · "I can't treat it as the complete population yet." · "I've limited the AccessGate conclusion…" · "I need your permission before sending these." Zobba never refers to itself in the third person inside the conversation.

Chrome is Zobba. The panel header, controls and warm surround follow the identity.
The page is the methodology's. Firm template, typeface, headings, reference scheme and colours. No Iris, no Hanken, no symbol in the body. Results say "Exception" in the firm's style.

Raeltec endorsement and Kobba

Endorsed lockup
Construction. "by Raeltec" in Hanken Grotesk 500 at 42% of the wordmark size, tertiary colour (#5E5A52; #B8B3A8 on dark). Gap = 0.28 × wordmark size, the same gap as symbol to wordmark.
