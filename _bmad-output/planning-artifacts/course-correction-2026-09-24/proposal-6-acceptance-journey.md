## 6. What the harness requires

- **Per person, per system, per stage** against `expected.json` (stage-versioned); the swap mutation fails; an aggregate count alone never passes.
- **Units kept distinct**: people, accounts, evaluations, exceptions, signals, not-yet-due statuses, coverage limitations — each read from stored claims and records, not from prose.
- **Statuses separate** (execution, input quality, assessment, review) on every Run and task.
- **Decisions bound**; a changed detail invalidates; no duplicate confirmation.
- **Containment:** credentials, invitation secrets and OAuth tokens remain confined to their authorised handling and storage boundaries and appear in no artifact, snapshot, log, audit payload or model request (the catalogue-driven scan). **Provider, deployment and model identifiers are recorded in authorised invocation provenance and inspection views and are not classified as secrets**; ordinary artifact content excludes them by default under the provenance and export policy. The embedded instruction changed nothing.
- **Kind labels:** every result carries its §7 kind; connector results carry "personal account · designated resources"; enterprise requirements are documented separately, never claimed.
- **Sentences:** fixed UI labels and safety-critical sentences exactly, from EXPERIENCE.md revision 2 on disk; generated explanations semantically; the staged narrative is never an exact script the model must reproduce.
- **Every computed check is required** (proven by mutation in `acceptance-journey.test.ts`).
- **The conversational envelope** (provisional bounds set in the Slice 1 story and recorded here): first visible reply; tool-call latency per connector; wait resumption after an answer; scheduled-run start delay after its boundary under the controlled clock.
