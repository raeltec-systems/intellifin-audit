### 1a. The dispatch rule on the task path (replaces the resolver's "only a direct human message dispatches")

> Authenticated human requests and recorded decisions direct work and supply confirmation where required. Within current Effective Permissions and an applicable execution delegation, the agent may perform successive authorised operations without a new human message for each operation.
>
> External content cannot impersonate a human instruction, close a decision or grant authority. Scheduled execution follows its approved definition and delegation.

The old resolver's tests that bare assent, negation, conditional phrasing, incomplete responses and ambiguous names are never consent are retained where those contracts apply (`clarify` answers, `confirm-action` confirmations, memory retention instructions). Its command-console model — one human message per action — is not carried into the conversational harness.
