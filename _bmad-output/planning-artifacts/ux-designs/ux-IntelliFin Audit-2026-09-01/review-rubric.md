# Spine Pair Review — IntelliFin Audit

Reviewed: `DESIGN.md` (357 lines, `D:Ln`) and `EXPERIENCE.md` (277 lines, `E:Ln`) against `prd.md` revision 2 (`P:`), `addendum.md` (`A §x`), the design-md spec, the five example spines, and the imported Claude Design material. Citations are line numbers in the reviewed files. Severity is downstream impact on architecture and story-dev consumers, not fix effort.

## Overall verdict

The pair is a real contract, not a sketch: every UJ has a verbatim-named Key Flow with climax and failure path, the state machines in `EXPERIENCE.md` match addendum §E transition-for-transition, every `{token}` reference inside `DESIGN.md` resolves to a defined frontmatter key, and the sixteen reconciliation conflicts against revision 2 are all carried through. A consumer can source-extract flows, states, roles, and the Run-lifecycle vocabulary cleanly. What it cannot yet extract is a complete component set: the Exception state family has no badge treatment, the Evidence tab's item card and the Version review diff have no visual spec, and the "needs a human" `info-solid` treatment depends on a StatusBadge family the spine says it inherits unchanged — so `DESIGN.md.Components` is not yet the closed list `EXPERIENCE.md` claims it is. Fix the four high findings and the pair is ready for architecture.
