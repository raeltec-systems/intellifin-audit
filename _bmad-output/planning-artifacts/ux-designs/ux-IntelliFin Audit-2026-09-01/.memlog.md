- (event) Created DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md workspace for IntelliFin Audit UX discovery.
- (decision by user) Product feel: calm, forensic, trustworthy, and audit-native; explicitly not chatbot-first, SIEM-like, RPA-like, or form-heavy traditional GRC.
- (decision by user) Primary mental model is Procedure → Run → Evidence → Result → Auditor Review.
- (decision by user) Overview must answer what ran, passed, failed, is inconclusive, needs attention, changed since the previous run, and whether evidence is complete and reliable.
- (decision by user) Run Detail is the anchor screen: procedure, population, Evidence Quality Gate, Exceptions, System Outcome, review status, and drill-down to source Evidence, compared values, applied rule, and exception rationale.
- (decision by user) Evidence provenance must be visually legible as source record → matched record → control rule → Exception.
- (decision by user) Evidence Quality Gate is prominent; incomplete, stale, contradictory, or unavailable Evidence displays Inconclusive with reasons and never presents a control conclusion.
- (decision by user) AI stays in the background; agent activity and session replay are supporting detail, while primary interaction uses Procedures, Evidence, Exceptions, Results, and Auditor Review.
- (decision by user) Human review should resemble deliberate audit-workpaper review, showing reviewer identity, time, and rationale or disagreement.
- (direction by user) Inspiration: Linear hierarchy/restraint/navigation; GitHub Actions runs/stages/history; Stripe density; Splunk or Sentinel drill-down; Vanta or Drata control-evidence relationships.
- (direction by user) Avoid ServiceNow or Archer form density, SIEM chart/alert walls, exposed RPA builders, chat-first UI, and excessive dashboards, heatmaps, or PoC configuration.
- (decision by user) PoC navigation stays small: Overview, Procedures, Runs, Run or Evidence Detail, Review, and minimal Administration.
- (direction by user) UX north star: experienced auditors immediately recognize how audit work is executed, evidenced, challenged, and reviewed; CAEs quickly answer what is failing, what changed, and whether Evidence is trustworthy.
- (decision by user) Use Design Handoff mode targeting Claude Design rather than the configured Google Stitch handoff.
- (decision by user) Confirmed UX sources: final product brief, final PRD, and PRD addendum; no additional brand assets supplied.
- (assumption) Claude Design handoff assumes a desktop-first responsive experience optimized for 1280–1600 px, usable at 1024 px, with narrow-screen reading support.
- (assumption) Claude Design handoff assumes a light-first PoC; dark mode is optional token-ready extension rather than a required mockup set.
- (assumption) Chief Audit Executive uses Overview and read-only detail surfaces; no separate executive dashboard is added.
