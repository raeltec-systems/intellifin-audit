## Limits

Local protocol fixtures establish owned signing/request/retry/read-back semantics, not live AWS service qualification, bucket versioning configuration, IAM/KMS permissions, retention policy, certificate-chain deployment setup, or live connector acquisition. No customer data, paid infrastructure or deployment was used. Direct upload verifies byte identity and custody; source fields remain attributed assertions, not proof of truth or completeness.

Execution results, exact commands, logs, screenshots and source freeze are recorded in the lead verification report and web/API reports. A named proof listed above must not be interpreted as passed until its execution result is recorded there.
