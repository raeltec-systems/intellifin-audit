## Limits

Automatic plain UTF-8 capture covers at most 16 KiB with exact byte locations and visible omissions. Retrieval returns at most fifty records after inspecting at most 1,024 candidates; partial/empty results do not establish absence. Exact lookup requires an already known reference. PDF/Office extraction and global discovery remain later consumers.

Methodology, skills and knowledge are independently checked inspections, not an atomic model-consumption snapshot. This story does not invoke models, execute skills, perform autonomous audit work or qualify a real managed computer. Local PostgreSQL/Chromium results do not qualify other database locales, browsers or native operating-system tabs.

No merge, deployment, later-story implementation, customer data or paid qualification is included. The batch handoff identifies the next dependency-ready work and preserves the unapproved early real-computer qualification proposal.
